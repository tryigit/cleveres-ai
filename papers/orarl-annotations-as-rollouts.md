# Annotations as Rollouts: Efficient and Scalable Reinforcement Learning for Video MLLMs

**TL;DR:** OraRL is a novel reinforcement learning framework designed to post-train Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) for video perception. It drastically improves sample efficiency by integrating dataset annotations as "oracle rollouts" into on-policy groups. This method resolves the "advantage inversion" problem, requires significantly less compute time compared to existing techniques like [Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)](../concepts/group-relative-policy-optimization.md), and scales effectively with model size, setting new benchmarks on spatial-intelligence tasks.

## The Challenge: RL Post-Training for Video Models

Post-training video MLLMs using traditional Reinforcement Learning (RL) on large multi-task datasets is computationally expensive and sample-inefficient. Existing methods often rely on sampling on-policy groups (generating multiple responses for the same prompt) to calculate advantages. However, generating high-quality rollouts, especially with computationally heavy Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning, yields very few strong positive examples, making optimization slow and costly.

## The Solution: OraRL and Oracle Rollouts

The researchers behind OraRL identified a critical, overlooked opportunity: the annotations (ground truth data) provided in the dataset can be used directly as positive optimization targets during RL.

Instead of just using annotations to score generated rollouts, OraRL injects the annotation into the on-policy group as an **oracle rollout**.

### Overcoming Advantage Inversion

A naive integration of a perfect "oracle" rollout into an on-policy group creates a problem called **advantage inversion**. Because the oracle score is so high, it artificially inflates the group's average baseline reward. As a result, otherwise good, positive policy rollouts are mathematically penalized because they fall below this artificially high average.

OraRL solves this with a **decoupled advantage estimator**:
1.  **Oracle-Free Baseline:** The baseline for the group is calculated *only* using the policy's own generated rollouts. This ensures fair evaluation of the model's genuine outputs.
2.  **Oracle-Policy Gap Modulation:** The gap between the oracle's reward and the policy's best reward is used to modulate a directional gain and a separate, detached advantage for the oracle itself.

### Sign-Balanced Pruning for Efficiency

To maximize computational efficiency, OraRL employs **sign-balanced pruning**. It retains only the oracle rollout and the strongest positive and negative rollouts generated by the policy, discarding the rest.

This dramatic reduction in compute means OraRL requires just **2.2x the step time of Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)**, which is less than half the time required by standard GRPO with CoT (4.9x).

## Performance & Scalability (August 2026)

OraRL demonstrates strong scalability with both model size (tested from 0.8B to 9B parameters) and dataset size (up to 100k prompts).

*   **Speed:** Without CoT overhead, Video-ORA-9B achieves rapid decoding speeds (130 ms compared to 4,780 ms for standard CoT approaches).
*   **Accuracy:** It significantly outperforms prior best models, raising temporal mIoU and segmentation scores.
*   **Benchmarks:** On the VSI-Bench, OraRL scores 73.1, vastly outperforming closed-source models like GPT-5 (55.0) and Gemini-3-Pro (55.1).

## Real-World Application & Who Should Care

### (Rocket) THE PERFORMANCE MONSTERS (SOTA Seekers):
AI researchers and engineers building next-generation multimodal models can use OraRL to aggressively scale video intelligence. The decoupled advantage estimator provides a mathematically rigorous way to force models to learn from perfect ground truth without destabilizing the RLHF/RLAIF process.

### (Money) THE COST & LATENCY OPTIMIZERS (API Developers):
For teams deploying video analysis APIs, OraRL is a massive cost-saver during the training phase. By cutting the step time down to 2.2x of SFT (compared to 4.9x for GRPO), you can align video models much faster and cheaper. Furthermore, the ability to achieve high accuracy *without* forcing slow CoT generation at inference time drops latency from ~4.8 seconds to ~130ms, directly impacting API throughput.

### (Person at Computer) THE EVERYDAY PROMPT ENGINEERS:
While everyday users won't run OraRL directly, the resulting models (like Video-ORA-9B) mean you will soon interact with video AI that understands precise temporal tracking and spatial relationships instantly, without having to wait for the model to "think out loud" for several seconds.
