# Qwen-Image-2.0-RL Technical Report

## TL;DR

Qwen-Image-2.0-RL introduces a post-training pipeline that applies [Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)](../concepts/rlhf.md) and on-policy distillation (OPD) to significantly improve both the visual quality and instruction-following capability of the Qwen-Image-2.0 diffusion model.

By constructing task-specific composite reward models and utilizing a scalable [Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)](../concepts/group-relative-policy-optimization.md) based RL training framework, the model achieves consistent gains in aesthetic quality, prompt adherence, and editing accuracy. It boasts Elo ratings of 1193 in the text-to-image arena (+78) and 1349 in the image edit arena (+93).

## The Methodology: RLHF and OPD

The pipeline operates in a sophisticated sequence designed to maximize model alignment with user intent:
1.  **Composite Reward Signals:** Reliable reward signals are generated by fine-tuning vision-language models using a pointwise scoring paradigm combined with chain-of-thought reasoning.
2.  **GRPO-Based RL Framework:** The framework incorporates a hybrid classifier-free guidance (CFG) strategy. This preserves pre-trained knowledge while introducing prompt curation (via intra-group reward range filtering) and per-category reward weight calibration.
3.  **On-Policy Distillation (OPD):** To merge the distinct task-specialized RL policies (for Text-to-Image and image editing), OPD serves as the final training stage. It consolidates multiple teacher models into a single student model by aligning trajectory-level velocity matching.

## The Reward System

The reward models are specialized based on the target task to ensure nuanced feedback:
*   **Text-to-Image Generation:** Focuses on alignment (does it match the prompt?), aesthetics (is it visually pleasing?), and portrait fidelity dimensions.
*   **Image Editing:** Prioritizes instruction-following accuracy (did it edit the exact area requested?) and face identity preservation.

## Real-World Application & Who Should Care

*   🚀 **THE PERFORMANCE MONSTERS (SOTA Seekers):** Researchers in generative AI can leverage the task-specialized RL policies and trajectory-level velocity matching through OPD to consolidate highly specific teacher models into a single, highly capable diffusion model.
*   💰 **THE COST & LATENCY OPTIMIZERS (API Developers):** The distilled single student model represents a significant efficiency win. By merging T2I and editing capabilities through OPD, developers can deploy one highly capable endpoint instead of maintaining separate specialized models, reducing VRAM overhead and inference latency.
*   💻 **THE EVERYDAY PROMPT ENGINEERS:** Users interacting with next-generation diffusion models will experience substantially better adherence to complex, multi-part prompts and precise editing instructions, resulting in fewer required retries.
